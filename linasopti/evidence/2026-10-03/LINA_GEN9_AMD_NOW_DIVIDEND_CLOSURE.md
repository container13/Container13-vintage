# Gen9 – AMD/NOW cash-dividend absence follow-up

2026-10-03 • CASH_DIVIDEND_ABSENCE_CLOSED_FOR_TARGET_RANGE_ALL_CA_STILL_OPEN

## AMD

AMD's own 2016 SEC prospectus states that AMD had never declared or paid cash dividends on its common stock. AMD's audited 2022 10-K then provides the complete 2020–2022 cash-flow/equity bridge without a dividend distribution and describes shareholder returns through repurchases. The audited 2024 10-K likewise describes 2023–2024 financing/shareholder returns without a dividend distribution, and AMD's current investor FAQ states that AMD does not currently pay a cash dividend.

Taken together, these issuer/SEC records close the cash-dividend absence question for the Gen9 target window 2020-01-01 through 2024-12-31. This does not by itself prove absence of every other corporate action.

## ServiceNow (NOW)

ServiceNow's 2021 10-K explicitly states that it had never declared or paid cash dividends. Its 2024 10-K states that expected dividend yield is zero because it has not declared dividends and does not currently intend to do so. This bridges the target range through 2024-12-31 and closes cash-dividend absence for the target window.

A later 5-for-1 ServiceNow stock split was approved/effected in December 2025, outside the Gen9 2020–2024 target period. It must not be projected backward as an in-period event, though later vendor-adjustment reference dates still require normal corporate-action lineage checks.

## Decision

For **cash dividends only**, AMD and NOW are upgraded to:
CASH_DIVIDEND_ABSENCE_CONFIRMED_FOR_TARGET_RANGE.

This is not an all-corporate-actions approval and does not approve the Gen9 dataset. No candidate prices, adjustment factors, verification flags, research state, Worker, Forward or trading state are changed.
